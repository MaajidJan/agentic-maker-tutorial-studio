---

### 🎬 Part 1: The Problem & The Big Idea (00:00 – 00:30)

### 🧠 Part 2: Multimodal Circuit Analysis & Agentic Reasoning (00:30 – 01:05)
**On Screen**: Uploading the circuit photo, pasting the prompt (e.g. Attack on Titan / Shonen / Bat Sonar theme), clicking Launch Pipeline, and watching Step 1 (Analyzer) & Step 2 (Scriptwriter) run.

> **Voiceover (00:30 – 01:05)**:  
> *"It all begins with our **Multimodal Analyzer Agent**. By feeding raw photos or video clips directly into **Gemini 3.5 Flash** using the official Google GenAI SDK, Botrix autonomously identifies the exact hardware components — like the Arduino Uno, HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor, and servo motor.*  
>  
> *It then translates complex technical principles into intuitive, kid-friendly analogies — comparing sonar radar waves to bat echolocation — and pinpoints four dramatic key moments across the video timeline."*

### 🎨 Part 3: Dynamic Real Reaction Stickers & Sound Design (01:05 – 01:45)
**On Screen**: Stepper moving to Asset Generation, showing dynamic search and rendering of reaction stickers (Mikasa focused glare, Eren shock alert, Luffy victory) and playing the audio wave cues.

> **Voiceover (01:05 – 01:45)**:  
> *"Next, our specialized agents collaborate in parallel.*  
>  
> *The **Reaction Sticker Engine** dynamically interprets the director's theme without any hardcoded constraints. It autonomously formulates targeted queries to find real iconic anime and pop-culture character moments live from the web, transforming them into high-res, die-cut vinyl stickers with emotion tags and drop shadows.*  
>  
> *Simultaneously, our **Sound Designer Agent** searches Freesound or procedurally synthesizes crystal-clear 44.1kHz audio effects — from futuristic power-up chimes to submarine sonar pings and victory fanfares."*

---

### 📜 Part 4: Scriptwriting & Interactive Human-in-the-Loop Review (01:45 – 02:25)
**On Screen**: Scrolling through the generated teleprompter dialogue, showing inline `[SFX]` and `[REACTION]` cue markers, and demonstrating the interactive "Request Revision" / "Approve" controls.

> **Voiceover (01:45 – 02:25)**:  
> *"Our **Scriptwriter Agent** composes a full teleprompter dialogue with embedded inline cues, perfectly synchronizing character reactions and sound effects at every timecode.*  
>

## 📋 Copy-Paste Full Voiceover Script (Continuous Text for AI TTS)

```text
Millions of students and makers build incredible electronics, Arduino robots, and IoT projects every day. But turning a raw circuit build into an engaging, broadcast-ready YouTube or TikTok tutorial is overwhelming. It takes hours of scripting, audio editing, and visual effects.

Meet Botrix — an autonomous multi-agent AI studio powered by Google Gemini 3.5 Flash that instantly transforms raw photos and videos of your hardware builds into complete, kid-friendly video tutorial packages in seconds.

It all begins with our Multimodal Analyzer Agent. By feeding raw photos or video clips directly into Gemini 3.5 Flash using the official Google GenAI SDK, Botrix autonomously identifies the exact hardware components — like the Arduino Uno, HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor, and servo motor.

It then translates complex technical principles into intuitive, kid-friendly analogies — comparing sonar radar waves to bat echolocation — and pinpoints four dramatic key moments across the video timeline.

Next, our specialized agents collaborate in parallel.

The Reaction Sticker Engine dynamically interprets the director's theme without any hardcoded constraints. It autonomously formulates targeted queries to find real iconic anime and pop-culture character moments live from the web, transforming them into high-res, die-cut vinyl stickers with emotion tags and drop shadows.

Simultaneously, our Sound Designer Agent searches Freesound or procedurally synthesizes crystal-clear 44.1kHz audio effects — from futuristic power-up chimes to submarine sonar pings and victory fanfares.

Our Scriptwriter Agent composes a full teleprompter dialogue with embedded inline cues, perfectly synchronizing character reactions and sound effects at every timecode.

Botrix incorporates a true Human-in-the-Loop Director Review loop. As the director, you can inspect every asset, listen to the audio cues, and give natural language feedback — like "Make the radar explanation punchier" — and the agentic pipeline instantly updates state.

All session state is continuously tracked and synced with Google Cloud Firestore for asynchronous persistence.

Once approved, Botrix exports a complete production bundle: structured JSON metadata, teleprompter markdown, high-res reaction PNGs, broadcast WAV audio, and a standalone interactive HTML player.

Architected for Google Cloud Run and Firestore, Botrix scales effortlessly from a local CLI tool to a global microservice.

Botrix empowers the next generation of educators, students, and creators to share the magic of electronics with the world. Built with Google Gemini.

Thank you!
```
